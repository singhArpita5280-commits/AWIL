How this works

City-wide air quality monitors are too sparse to catch localized events — a burning pile in one lane, a smoky stretch of road. AirWatch lets residents flag what they see, uses Gemini's vision model to classify each photo, and clusters nearby reports into hotspots on the map.

Authorities get a triage view ranked by severity, and Gemini can draft a short advisory for any hotspot in one click — turning scattered citizen reports into an actionable, real-time picture of hyperlocal air quality across a city.
